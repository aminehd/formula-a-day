# a formula a day

I made a framework for JAX visualisation — you write a function, it animates
every intermediate value. Then I figured out you can make really beautiful
images with it.

I think if you could *see* your craft, it just adds one more stream of data.
Knowing what my formula looks like seems good for humans — and maybe as an
agentic knowledge base too. Imagine a proof agent that can reason in shape and
form.

One ML building block a day: the formula, what it does, and every step animated.
